# Nest Egg

Nest Egg shows a household what it owns today and when it can retire.

People: name, birth year. Accounts: name, type (cash, brokerage, retirement, home, other asset, mortgage, other debt), owner. A monthly snapshot records every account's balance; net worth is assets minus debts. A plan: retirement age per person, monthly saving until then, monthly spending after, yearly return and inflation, one-off events. Kids' savings: monthly deposit per child, target age, return.

Screens: net worth with its history, snapshot entry, when can we retire, compare plans, kids' savings. Money is US dollars in whole cents. Projections say "A projection, not financial advice".
