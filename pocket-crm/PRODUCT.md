# Pocket CRM

A CRM for a freelancer.

Contacts: name, email, phone, company, tags. Deals: title, contact, value in cents, stage (lead, qualified, proposal, won, lost). Activities: note, call, meeting, email, task.

Screens: dashboard, contacts with search, a contact page with a timeline, a deals board with a Move menu, and quick search with Ctrl+K.

Fake data only.
